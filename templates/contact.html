FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions before reaching out.

You can track your order in real-time by visiting the "My Orders" section in your account dashboard. Each order has a unique tracking number that you can use to monitor delivery status, estimated arrival time, and current location of your delivery partner.

We accept all major payment methods including credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and cash on delivery. All transactions are processed securely through encrypted payment gateways.

Orders can be cancelled within 15 minutes of placing them from the "My Orders" section. If the order has already been processed or dispatched, please contact our support team immediately for assistance with the cancellation.

To request a refund, please contact our support team through the contact form or call us directly. Provide your order details and the reason for the refund. We process refunds within 5-7 business days after approval.

You can reach our customer support team via the contact form on this page, by emailing kamalarkaj11@gmail.com, or by calling +91-89885-45540. Our team is available Monday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Our delivery hours are from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before the cutoff time in your area. You can select your preferred delivery slot during checkout.
